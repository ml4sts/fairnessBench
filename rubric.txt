RUBRIC:

1. Model Overview
   4 = Clear purpose, scope, and context defined with specific objectives.
   3 = Well‑defined but could be more specific.
   2 = Vague or incomplete definition of purpose and scope.
   1 = No clear definition of purpose or scope.

2. Stakeholder Identification and Fairness Definition
   4 = Diverse stakeholders identified with specific fairness goals set.
   3 = Most stakeholders identified, goals mostly clear.
   2 = Limited stakeholder involvement and fairness goals.
   1 = No stakeholder involvement or unclear fairness goals.

3. Data Collection and Processing
   4 = Comprehensive, representative datasets with privacy considerations without compromising fairness.
   3 = Datasets generally representative, some gaps; privacy concerns considered but some trade‑offs.
   2 = Some missing or unbalanced datasets; key subgroups and trade‑offs not sufficiently addressed.
   1 = Data collection not reflective of population or privacy concerns ignored.

4. Bias Detection and Mitigation
   4 = Comprehensive bias mitigation strategies (preprocessing, post‑processing, fair representation learning) implemented.
   3 = Some bias mitigation strategies used with minor gaps.
   2 = Only basic bias mitigation approaches used.
   1 = No bias mitigation strategies used.

5. Fairness Metric Selection
   4 = Clear, context‑specific metrics chosen with transparent, actionable decisions.
   3 = Good selection of metrics with reasonable transparency.
   2 = Metrics chosen without full alignment with context.
   1 = No clear fairness metrics or criteria selection.

6. Model Selection and Training
   4 = Fairness mitigation techniques incorporated from the start.
   3 = Some fairness techniques applied, but not comprehensive.
   2 = Techniques implemented too late in the process.
   1 = No fairness techniques applied during training.

7. Evaluation and Testing
   4 = Thorough disaggregated evaluation and regular bias auditing.
   3 = Adequate evaluation with some gaps in auditing.
   2 = Evaluation done for limited groups; auditing irregular.
   1 = No evaluation or auditing conducted.
